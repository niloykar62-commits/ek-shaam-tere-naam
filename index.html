The words

Written for this page. The mukhda is the refrain and comes back at the end. The key is D, the tempo 72 beats a minute, and the tune is played by synthesized strings with a tanpura-style drone underneath.

Play the film with a song of your own

Pick an audio file from your own device and the film will play along to it. If you also type the words, they are shown one line at a time and spread evenly across the song. Everything stays on your device and nothing is uploaded.
